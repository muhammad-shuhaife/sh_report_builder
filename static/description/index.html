Before you install

  • Platform: Odoo 20, on-premise or Odoo.sh. Custom Python addons cannot be installed on standard Odoo Online.
  • Dependencies: Base and Web. The Website app is not needed.
  • PDF rendering: a configured, working Odoo PDF engine is required. Banner repeat controls were tested with patched wkhtmltopdf.
  • Python: ReportLab and Pillow must be available in the Odoo environment.
  • Access: report design and publishing are limited to Settings administrators.
  • Business models: ready-made choices depend on installed apps. Sales, Invoices, Purchase Orders and Delivery Slips require their respective business apps; custom persistent models can also be selected.
  • Preview: uses an accessible sample record. New reports must be saved once; edits inside block dialogs appear after Save & Close.
  • Data: report rendering stays on your Odoo server. No external rendering subscription or activation key.
